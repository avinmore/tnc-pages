# Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 9 October 2026

Rutuma – Period & Ovulation Tracker ("Rutuma", "we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy and safeguarding your personal and health-related data.

This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our mobile application.

---

## 1. Data Storage — Local Only

**All your personal and health data is stored exclusively on your device.** Rutuma does not transmit, upload, or store your data on any external server or cloud service. We do not have access to your data at any time.

This includes:
- Period dates and cycle length
- Ovulation and fertility information
- Symptoms and mood tracking
- Medicine reminders and logs
- Personal notes
- App preferences and settings

We do **not** collect or store your email address, login credentials, or any personally identifiable account information on our servers.

---

## 2. Authentication

Rutuma supports optional sign-in via Apple or Google for convenience. Authentication is handled entirely by Apple and Google respectively. We do not receive, store, or have access to your passwords or account credentials. Authentication tokens are stored securely on your device using the iOS Keychain or Android's encrypted storage.

---

## 3. Analytics & Crash Reporting

To improve app stability and performance, Rutuma uses **Firebase Analytics** and **Firebase Crashlytics** (provided by Google). These services may collect:

- Device model and operating system version
- App version
- Anonymous usage events (e.g., feature interactions, session duration)
- Crash logs and diagnostic data

This data is **anonymized** and does not include your health data, personal identity, or any information you enter into the app.

We do **not** collect or track your IP address, geographic location, or any location-derived data.

We do not use this data for advertising purposes.

---

## 4. In-App Purchases

Rutuma offers optional premium features via subscription. All payments are processed exclusively through the **Apple App Store** (iPhone) or **Google Play** (Android). We do not collect, process, or store any payment or billing information.

---

## 5. Data Backup & Transfer

Rutuma does not have servers that store your health data. Everything you log stays on your device unless you choose to back it up. You can back up your data in two ways:

- **Backup file** — Create an **encrypted backup file** (.rutuma) on your device and save or share it wherever you choose, such as iCloud Drive, Google Drive, the Files app, AirDrop or email. Rutuma never receives a copy of this file.
- **Google Sign-In & Sync (optional)** — If you sign in with Google, Rutuma saves your encrypted backup directly to **your own Google Drive account**, in a private app folder that only Rutuma can access. Rutuma cannot see your other Google Drive files, and your backup is never sent to Rutuma's servers or to anyone else. Google Sign-In also provides your name and email address, which are used only to show which account is connected.
- **Removing a Google Drive backup** — Signing out only disconnects your account; your backup stays in your Google Drive so you can restore it later. To remove it, use **Delete All Data** in the app while signed in, or go to Google Drive → Settings → Manage apps → Rutuma → Delete hidden app data. You can revoke Rutuma's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Rutuma's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

**Important:** Since all data is stored locally, if you delete the app or lose your device without creating a backup, your data cannot be recovered. Team Rutuma has no ability to retrieve your data.

## 7. Your Rights & Control

Since all data resides on your device, you have full control:

- **View your data** — All information is visible within the app.
- **Export your data** — Use the built-in export feature (CSV, JSON, or PDF formats).
- **Delete your data** — Delete the app to remove all data permanently, or use in-app settings to clear specific data.
- **Backup your data** — Use the encrypted backup feature before deleting. **Delete All Data** in the app also removes your Google Drive backup if you are signed in.

No request to Team Rutuma is needed to exercise these rights — you are in complete control.

For any questions or concerns, contact us at: **support@rutuma.com**

## 8. Advertising & Tracking

Rutuma does **not** engage in cross-app tracking.

- The app may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Premium and Ads-free users do not see ads.
- Advertisements are **not** based on your health data. Your health data is never shared with advertisers.
- On iPhone, we use Apple's App Tracking Transparency (ATT) framework. You will be asked for explicit permission before any cross-app tracking occurs. On Android, ads may use your device's advertising ID; you can reset it or opt out of personalized ads in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads).
- You can manage tracking preferences in your device settings.
